01 · Use

What you do

Three steps. Same labels as in the app.

  1. 01 · Plan Library

    Open Studio Apartment, 2-Bed Home, or Small Office — each marked SAMPLE — or Import a JSON plan / Create your own.

  2. 02 · Preview

    Check the top-down 2D plan, room sizes, and layout. Then tap Walk Through.

  3. 03 · Walk Through

    The plan sits on your floor at 1:1. Adjust Wall opacity, switch Full height or Knee-high outline, then move, rotate, or Reset.

02 · Audience

Who it’s for

People who need to feel a layout before committing.

Remodel See whether a wall move or open plan still fits the space you have.

Build Walk a sample or imported plan at true size before framing starts.

Furniture Check clearances and circulation with the plan’s furniture blocks in place.

The windows you’ll use
