How it works

Three steps from outline to a full-scale walk-through in your space.

Open a plan

Start with one of three labeled sample plans, import a JSON floor plan, or sketch a simple room in-app.

Preview in 2D

Check the top-down outline, confirm the layout, then place it in your surroundings for mixed reality.

Walk at 1:1

Step through walls at true scale with opacity controls, knee-high outline mode, and move, rotate, and reset.

Built for spatial clarity

Everything you need to preview a layout—nothing that tracks you.

True 1:1 mixed reality

Experience rooms at full scale so proportions, circulation, and furniture clearances feel real before construction.

100% offline

No account. No cloud sync. No tracking. Plans stay on your device—Roomplanr works without a network.

Import or create

Bring JSON floor plans or build simple room outlines in-app. Sample plans ship clearly labeled for quick demos.

Walk-through controls

Adjust wall opacity, switch to knee-high outlines, and move, rotate, or reset placement while you explore.

Visualization only

Roomplanr is for spatial visualization. Dimensions are approximate and not survey-accurate. Do not use Roomplanr for construction, legal boundaries, permitting, or precise measurement.
