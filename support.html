FAQ

How do I get started?

Open Roomplanr on Apple Vision Pro and choose one of the three clearly labeled sample plans. Use the 2D top-down preview to confirm the layout, then enter the 1:1 mixed-reality walk-through. From there you can adjust wall opacity, switch to knee-high outline mode, and move, rotate, or reset the plan in your space.

How do I import a JSON floor plan?

Use the in-app import option and select a JSON floor plan from your device. After import, review the 2D preview, then open the full-scale walk-through. You can also create simple room plans directly in the app without importing.

Does Roomplanr work offline? What about privacy?

Yes. Roomplanr works 100% offline—no account and no tracking. Plans are stored locally on your device. See our privacy policy for details.

Are the dimensions accurate?

No—not for surveying or construction. Roomplanr is for spatial visualization only. Dimensions are approximate and not survey-accurate. Do not rely on Roomplanr for building, legal boundaries, permitting, or precise measurement.
