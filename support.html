FAQ

How do I get started?

Open Roomplanr and go to Plan Library. Choose one of the SAMPLE plans — Studio Apartment, 2-Bed Home, or Small Office. Review the 2D preview, then tap Walk Through. From the control strip you can set Wall opacity, switch Full height or Knee-high outline, and move, rotate, or Reset the plan in your space.

How do I import a JSON floor plan?

In Plan Library, tap Import and select a JSON floor plan from your device. Review the 2D preview, then open Walk Through. You can also tap Create to sketch a simple plan with rooms side-by-side (dimensions in meters).

Does Roomplanr work offline?

Yes. Roomplanr works offline with no account and no tracking. Plans stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

Are the dimensions accurate?

No — not for surveying or construction. Roomplanr is for visualization only. Dimensions are approximate and not survey-accurate. Do not rely on it for building, legal boundaries, permitting, or precise measurement.
