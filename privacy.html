Privacy

Roomplanr is offline-first. We do not collect your data.

Summary

Roomplanr for Apple Vision Pro is designed to work entirely on your device. There is no account system, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. Roomplanr does not collect personal information, usage analytics, crash reports sent to us, advertising identifiers, or location data.

Where your plans live

Floor plans you open, import, or create are stored locally on your Apple Vision Pro. They are not uploaded to Roomplanr servers—because Roomplanr does not operate a cloud service for plans.

Network access

The app is built to run offline. Viewing sample plans, previewing layouts, and walking through rooms at 1:1 scale does not require an internet connection or an account.

Third parties

We do not share data with advertisers, analytics vendors, or other third parties, because we do not collect it.

Children

Roomplanr does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page. The nature of the product— offline, no account, no tracking—is core to how Roomplanr works.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@roomplanr.com
