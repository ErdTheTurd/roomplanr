Privacy

Privacy

Roomplanr works offline. We do not collect your data.

Summary

Roomplanr for Apple Vision Pro runs on your device. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. Roomplanr does not collect personal information, usage analytics, crash reports sent to us, advertising identifiers, or location data.

Where your plans live

Floor plans you open, import, or create are stored locally on your Apple Vision Pro. They are not uploaded to Roomplanr servers — Roomplanr does not operate a cloud service for plans.

Network access

The app is built to run offline. Viewing sample plans, previewing layouts, and walking through rooms at 1:1 scale does not require an internet connection or an account.

Third parties

We do not share data with advertisers, analytics vendors, or other third parties, because we do not collect it.

Children

Roomplanr does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Offline, no account, and no tracking are part of how Roomplanr works.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@roomplanr.com
